## What shipped

Seeded provisioning + magic-link sign-in (uniform anti-enumeration flow), four DB-read roles behind the `requireRole` chokepoint, `/admin/users` with transactional audit (`UserEvent`, append-only) and a race-safe last-admin guard, deactivation as a total kill-switch, three-window sign-in throttle, idempotent seed with loud-failure bootstrap invariant. 67 tests, all integration tests executing against real Postgres locally and in CI.

## What surprised us

1. **The race test passed with the lock deleted.** Two "concurrent" transactions serialised by timing luck, so the guard's test was decoration until the engineer added a test-only barrier seam that parks tx1 inside the race window — then proved red-without-lock empirically. Falsifiability checking caught it; timing-based concurrency tests would not have.
2. **The throttle arithmetic was wrong and survived two gates.** 30/hour "protecting" a 100/day quota is 720/day; engineer implemented it, reviewer approved it, the advisor caught it. Rate limits need quota arithmetic (max sustained rate × quota period), not vibes.
3. **The seed had a bootstrap deadlock.** Promote-without-reactivate could complete with zero active admins — recoverable only by raw SQL against prod. Surfaced as a reviewer nit, upgraded by the advisor to a loud-failure condition.
4. **Middleware matcher exclusions are prefix matches.** `signin` also excluded `/signin-foo` — a silent-public class, now anchored. Corollary: `/signin/*` is deliberately a public namespace; future routes there ship unauthenticated.
5. **The gitignored `.env` held the production DATABASE_URL** (ops convenience from provisioning) and Prisma autoloads `.env` — one bare `prisma migrate dev` from prod damage. Mitigated by explicit per-command URL overrides in the engineer brief; a standing hazard worth codifying.
6. **Preview deployments have no env at all** (prod-scoped vars only): no prod-leak risk, but PR previews are non-functional until preview-scoped `DATABASE_URL`/`AUTH_*` exist. Noted; not needed yet.

## What we'd do differently

- Concurrency guards get a deterministic race window from the start; "passed the race test" means nothing until red-without-lock is proven once.
- Rate-limit design states the quota arithmetic in the design doc, not just the window numbers.
- Seeds/bootstrap scripts assert their post-condition (here: ≥1 active admin) and exit non-zero otherwise — silent idempotency is not a virtue when the invariant fails.
